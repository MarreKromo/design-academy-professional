# Design Academy — Roadmap v1.0

48 planned lessons in six modules. Sequence and pace may change following assessments. All lessons begin as NOT STARTED.

## Module 1 — Fundamentals (01–08)
01. Visual hierarchy
02. Contrast and emphasis
03. Alignment and spacing
04. Grids and composition
05. Typography foundations
06. Color theory
07. Visual analysis and references
08. Fundamentals assessment: three posters

## Module 2 — Tools and production (09–16)
09. Figma interface and frames
10. Shapes, vectors and paths
11. Components and styles
12. Raster vs vector and file formats
13. Photopea layers and masks
14. Retouching and image correction
15. Export, resolution and organization
16. Production assessment

## Module 3 — Brand identity (17–24)
17. Brand strategy and brief
18. Audience and positioning
19. Moodboards and art direction
20. Logo design principles
21. Logo systems and variations
22. Color and typography systems
23. Brand guidelines
24. Fictional identity assessment

## Module 4 — Advertising (25–32)
25. Campaign concepts
26. Copy and visual storytelling
27. Product photography direction
28. Ad layout and adaptation
29. Social formats
30. Web and e-commerce graphics
31. Critique and iteration
32. Multi-format campaign assessment

## Module 5 — Packaging and print (33–40)
33. Packaging fundamentals
34. Label hierarchy
35. Dielines and dimensions
36. Print color: RGB and CMYK
37. Bleed, margins and preflight
38. Mockups and presentation
39. Production handoff
40. Packaging assessment

## Module 6 — Portfolio and business (41–48)
41. Portfolio selection
42. Case study writing
43. Client discovery and briefs
44. Scope, estimates and pricing
45. Feedback and revision management
46. Usage rights and deliverables
47. Simulated client project
48. Final portfolio presentation and assessment
