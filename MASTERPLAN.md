# Design Academy — Masterplan v1.0

Start: 2026-11-05  
Status: ACTIVE — setup

## Vision
Develop independent professional graphic-design skills, apply them to Kromolicki Pure Essence, and build a portfolio suitable for future paid client work.

## Starting point
Beginner; Chromebook; 5–7 hours per week. Planned duration: 12 months, 48 lessons. Pace is adjustable; professional competence is assessed, not guaranteed by time spent.

## Learning outcomes
1. Visual communication: hierarchy, composition, typography, color, grids.
2. Production: vector and raster design, formats, exports and print preparation.
3. Branding: strategy, identity systems, guidelines and consistency.
4. Commercial design: campaigns, packaging, client briefs and presentations.
5. Professional practice: portfolio, feedback, pricing, licensing and handoff.

## Tools
Start with Figma in Chrome. Introduce Photopea and vector tools when appropriate. Avoid unnecessary subscriptions. Blender and 3D are optional later subjects.

## Assessment
Every lesson includes theory, guided practice, independent work, review, revision and PASS/FAIL. PASS requires demonstrated understanding and usable deliverables; no automatic approvals. Approved masters remain unchanged; revisions receive new version numbers.

## Brand integrity
Do not alter original Kromolicki Pure Essence packaging, labels, logo, emblem or typography without explicit permission. Distinguish practice mockups from official brand assets.

## Source of truth
GitHub documents record plans and verified progress. ChatGPT supports teaching and feedback; Figma stores editable design work. Never invent completed tasks or approvals.
